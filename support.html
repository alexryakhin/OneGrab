Frequently asked questions

How do I save a link?

Copy a link from TikTok, Threads, Instagram, or another supported app, open OneGrab, and tap Save from Clipboard. Customize your save, then keep it in Photos — or share it right away.

Which platforms are supported?

OneGrab works with many services, including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, X (Twitter), Facebook, Reddit, and more. If a link isn’t supported, the app will let you know.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open OneGrab, tap the settings (gear) icon, then Restore purchase. Your subscription will be restored if it’s still active.

Saving isn’t working. What should I try?

Make sure you’ve copied a direct link (not a general profile or feed URL). Check your internet connection and try again. If it still fails, email us with the link and we’ll look into it.
